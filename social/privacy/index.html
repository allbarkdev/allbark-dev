All Bark Social Tools Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

All Bark (“we”, “us”) uses a small set of private tools to publish our own short videos to our own TikTok account and YouTube channel. These tools are not offered to the public and nobody else signs in to them.

What the tools access

DataWhy
Sign-in tokens for All Bark’s own TikTok and YouTube accountsTo upload videos we made, as drafts or posts, after we approve each one.
Basic account info for those accounts (account name and ID)To confirm the video goes to the right account.
The videos, captions and titles we uploadTo publish them.

The tools do not read other people’s accounts, followers, messages, comments or viewing data. We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share any data.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in our private build environment and are used only to make upload requests to TikTok and Google. Videos are stored in our own project files before upload.

Removing access

We can revoke the tools’ access at any time from TikTok’s and Google’s account settings, which immediately stops them working. Google’s use of data obtained through its APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
