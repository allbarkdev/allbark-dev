Cerberus Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Cerberus is a character builder and table sheet for fifth edition games, made by All Bark (“we”, “us”). Most of it never talks to a server at all. This page explains what does leave your device, who handles it, and how to get rid of it.

Players don’t need an account. Your heroes, dice rolls, homebrew packs and settings are stored on your device and never sent to us. Playing at a table on the same Wi-Fi goes device to device, not through us.

We do not collect your location, contacts or browsing history. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking, and we don’t sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Online tables

When a DM takes a table online, the DM’s device and each player’s device connect to our relay. The relay passes the table’s messages between them without reading or saving them; they only exist in its memory while the table is open, and it keeps no log of what was said. The relay sees each device’s IP address to hold the connection, and briefly counts wrong table codes from an address to slow down guessing; neither is written down. Its logs contain only how many tables and seats are open.

Deleting your data

In Cerberus, open Settings → Account → Delete account. That deletes your DM account, your email and your subscription record from our servers straight away. Your heroes stay on your device, since they were never on ours; delete the app to remove them. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a store subscription, so cancel that in your device’s subscription settings first.

Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days and then deleted. They aren’t linked to an account, so they can’t be looked up by person.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing the address below. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

DM accounts are not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we’ll delete it. Players of any age can use Cerberus without an account.

Security

Everything between the app, our relay and our servers is encrypted in transit, and an account’s data needs that account’s sign-in to read. We keep the number of services holding anything deliberately small.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
