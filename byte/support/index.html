Byte Support

Byte is a budgeting app made by All Bark. If something isn't working, a receipt scanned wrong, or you just have a question, we'd like to hear about it.

Email support@allbark.dev and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days. If it's about a receipt, a screenshot of it helps a lot.

Subscriptions

Byte Pro is billed through your Apple ID, so Apple handles payment and refunds.

  1. Manage or cancel: in Byte, open Tag (your avatar, top right) and tap Manage subscription. You can also go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  2. New phone or reinstalled? Tap Restore purchases in Tag or on the paywall.
  3. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your account

In Byte, open Tag, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. This removes your account and data and disconnects any linked banks. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that first. See the privacy policy for exactly what is removed.

Privacy and data requests

For a copy of your data, corrections or deletion requests, email privacy@allbark.dev.
